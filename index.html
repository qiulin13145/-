Learning to Optimize

Optimization + Learning

I am interested in learning optimization algorithms from data while preserving the mathematical structures that make them reliable and generalizable. My work on decentralized optimization explores how convergence principles can constrain and guide learned optimizers.

IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing

02

LLM Efficient Pretraining

Efficient LLM

I study parameter- and memory-efficient pretraining for large language models: how to reduce the training footprint without giving up the optimization capacity needed at scale. I am particularly interested in low-rank structure, optimizer states, and training dynamics.

Preprint · 2025

Scalable Parameter and Memory Efficient Pretraining for LLM: Recent Algorithmic Advances and Benchmarking

Athanasios Glentis, Jiaxiang Li, Qiulin Shang†, Andi Han, Ioannis Tsaknakis, Quan Wei, Mingyi Hong

† co-first author

My current focus is AI-driven algorithm design: turning research ideas into executable search spaces, building scalable experimental loops, and designing evaluations that let AI iterate on algorithms rather than merely propose plausible heuristics.

About & Philosophy

I like turning vague ideas into things we can actually test.

A recurring theme in my research is to replace hand-designed intuition with structured, inspectable experimental loops: define the right state, expose meaningful algorithmic actions, control the cost of exploration, and ask whether an improvement survives outside the setting where it was discovered.
