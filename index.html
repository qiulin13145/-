About

Building systematic ways to discover better training algorithms.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at Peking University's Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies. My research centers on automated algorithm design for AI, with particular interest in optimization, parameter-efficient training, model efficiency, and the evaluation methodology needed to tell whether an algorithmic improvement is real and transferable.

A recurring theme in my work is to turn what would traditionally be a hand-designed heuristic into a structured and testable search problem: define the state, expose meaningful algorithmic actions, control experimental cost, and evaluate whether a discovered method generalizes across scales and settings.

Reliable Algorithm Evaluation

Designing low-cost evaluation protocols that distinguish genuine algorithmic gains from configuration, scale, seed, or benchmark artifacts.
